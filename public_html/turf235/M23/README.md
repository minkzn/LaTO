# M23 Final Exam Practice

74 questions (45 module + 29 textbook) covering topdressing and rolling: the five purposes of topdressing, its origin on golf and bowling greens, hand vs. mechanical application and localized dry spots, traditional soil-sand-peat blends vs. sand topdressing, topdressing after core cultivation, sand fingers and layering from reincorporated cores, topdressing quantity and light exclusion, thatch-control amount vs. frequency, resiliency and the thatch-sand proportion, matting with steel mats, brushes, and smooth rakes, sand layers over dense (Penn A-4) thatch, compressed thatch and layered profiles, weekly topdressing and bypass channels, and rolling for establishment, play repair, history, and green speed. The Textbook sub-test covers Ch. 6, pp. 201-207: Rolling, Topdressing, and Matting. The Overview page (scenario, objectives, readings) is not quizzed. Answer options are length-balanced so the longest choice is not a giveaway. 10 at a time.
