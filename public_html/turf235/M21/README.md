# M21 Final Exam Practice

95 questions from the module pages covering mowing: purposes and history (Budding's 1830 reel mower), shearing vs. impact cutting, reel/rotary/flail mowers, spin and relief grinds, bedknife adjustment, triplex and hover mowers, clip of the reel, marcelling and restitution height (with worked %V problems), effective height vs. bench setting, axillary bud responses, scalping, blade sharpness, clippings, mowing frequency and the one-third rule, and mowing pattern. The Overview page (scenario, objectives, readings) is not quizzed. Textbook enrichment (Ch. 5, pp. 141-151) is not included yet. Answer options are length-balanced so the longest choice is not a giveaway. 10 at a time.
