# M16 Exam 2 Practice

104 questions (79 from the module pages + 25 textbook enrichment from Ch. 4, pp. 93-111) covering climatology, topography, light (PAR, compensation and saturation points, morning vs. afternoon shade, phytochrome, light exclusion, photoperiod), temperature and heat transfer, ice damage and the species × hardening tables, moisture (transpiration, boundary layer, dew), and wind (pressure systems, black vs. white ice, winter covers). Overview pages (scenario, objectives, readings) are not quizzed. Question count follows the density of the material, and answer options are length-balanced so the longest choice is not a giveaway. 10 at a time.
