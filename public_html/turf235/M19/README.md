# M19 Exam 2 Practice

161 questions (127 from the module pages + 34 textbook enrichment from Ch. 5, pp. 151-170) covering the nutrient balance, soil testing, fertilizer analysis and grade, N carriers and their release mechanisms, growth response, N/P/K fate, liming, nutrient sufficiency ranges and deficiency symptoms, soil pH, and the M19.17 practice problems. Overview pages (scenario, objectives, readings) are not quizzed. Question count follows the density of the material, and answer options are length-balanced so the longest choice is not a giveaway. 10 at a time.
