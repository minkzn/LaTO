# M17 Exam 2 Practice

122 questions (91 from the module pages + 31 textbook enrichment from Ch. 4, pp. 111-139) covering soil physical and chemical properties, the USDA separates, pore space and water tables, CEC and pH, nutrient forms, the thatch zone experiments, thatch vs. soil, topdressing and mat, soil water and USGA root-zone specs, and black layer with the California and Purr-Wick designs. Overview pages (scenario, objectives, readings) are not quizzed. Question count follows the density of the material, and answer options are length-balanced so the longest choice is not a giveaway. 10 at a time.
